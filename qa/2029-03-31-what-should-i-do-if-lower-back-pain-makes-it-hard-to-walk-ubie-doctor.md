What should I do if lower back pain makes it hard to walk?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Lower back pain that makes walking difficult is often caused by spinal conditions such as lumbar spinal stenosis, herniated discs, sciatica, or degenerative disc disease. These conditions can compress nerves, reduce mobility, and cause pain that worsens with movement. Identifying the underlying cause is essential for choosing the right treatment, which may range from physical therapy and medication to injections or surgery.
