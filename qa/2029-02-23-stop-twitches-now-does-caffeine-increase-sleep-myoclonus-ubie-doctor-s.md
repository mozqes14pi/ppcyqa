Published on: 7/21/2026

Does caffeine increase the frequency of sleep myoclonus?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Does caffeine cause sleep myoclonus? Current research does not clearly establish a direct link between caffeine and sleep myoclonus (sudden muscle jerks during sleep). However, caffeine is a well-known stimulant that can disrupt sleep quality, delay sleep onset, and potentially worsen underlying sleep disturbances in sensitive individuals.

Because sleep myoclonus can stem from many causes—including stress, medications, neurological conditions, or other sleep disorders—identifying the true trigger requires a closer look at your symptoms.
