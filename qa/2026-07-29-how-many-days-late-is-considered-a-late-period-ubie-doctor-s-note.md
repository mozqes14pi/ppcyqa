Explanation

Most women have cycles that last between 21 and 35 days. That means if you normally have a 28-day cycle and your period hasn’t started by day 35, it would be defined as late. Small changes of a few days in your cycle from month to month can be normal and is not necessarily a sign of a more serious problem. Many things can change your cycle including stress, exercise habits, or changes in your weight and overall health. If your period is typically regular and is delayed by a full week or more, it could be a good idea to keep a diary of your cycle and talk with a doctor.
