Types of asthma treatments

The main downside to asthma is that it will likely never go away once you have it. Your doctor will advise you of asthma treatments that can ease the symptoms and can help prevent asthma attacks.

Inhaled corticosteroids are the most common asthma treatment and should be taken every day. This medicine can help prevent the swelling of airways which can lead to asthma attacks. In taking this medicine, you will use an inhaler to incorporate the medicine into your lungs.

Another popular asthma treatment is Leukotriene modifiers which can be taken orally. This medication can block leukotriene, which is things found in your body that can trigger asthma attacks.

Bronchodilators can also be used to combat the symptoms of asthma and can be introduced to your body through inhalers. This type of medicine can relax the muscle band found in your airways to prevent it from constricting.
