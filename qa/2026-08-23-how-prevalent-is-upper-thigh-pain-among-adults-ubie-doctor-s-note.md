Explanation

Upper thigh pain in adults can be caused by a condition called meralgia paresthetica. This condition happens when a nerve in the thigh, known as the lateral femoral cutaneous nerve, gets squeezed or pinched. This can lead to feelings of tingling, numbness, or burning pain on the outer part of the thigh.

Causes

Tight clothing: Wearing tight belts or pants can put pressure on the nerve.

Weight gain: Extra weight can increase the pressure on the nerve.
