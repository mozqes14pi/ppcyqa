Severe lower back pain when standing up is often linked to poor posture, muscle strain, or underlying spinal issues such as herniated discs, sciatica, or spinal stenosis. Key causes include:

Muscle fatigue or strain from prolonged sitting or improper lifting

Postural imbalance that stresses the lumbar spine

Spinal conditions like disc degeneration or nerve compression

Explanation

Experiencing severe lower back pain when standing up from a sitting position can be concerning. According to the provided references, this type of pain can be related to how you move and your posture. Poor posture and certain movements can strain the lower back, leading to pain (Reference 1). Additionally, conditions like spinal stenosis, which is a narrowing of spaces within your spine, can also cause pain and discomfort (Reference 2).

To address this pain, consider the following steps:

Check Your Posture: Ensure you are sitting and standing with good posture. This means keeping your back straight and shoulders back.

Move Regularly: Try not to sit for too long without moving. Taking short breaks to stand up and stretch can help.

Consult a Professional: If the pain persists, it is important to see a healthcare professional. They can provide a proper diagnosis and suggest appropriate treatments.
