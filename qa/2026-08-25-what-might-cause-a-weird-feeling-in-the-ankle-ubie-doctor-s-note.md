Explanation

A strange sensation in the ankle can arise from various causes. One potential reason is related to the sacroiliac joint, which can cause leg symptoms that might be felt in the ankle. This joint is located in the lower back, and problems with it can lead to discomfort or odd feelings in the legs.

Another possible cause is a past injury to the Achilles tendon. Even two years after an acute injury, people can still experience major functional deficits, which might include unusual sensations or feelings in the ankle area.

Additionally, engaging in activities like bungee jumping can also lead to symptoms in the ankle. The physical impact from such activities can cause different kinds of sensations or discomfort, which might be described as weird feelings.
