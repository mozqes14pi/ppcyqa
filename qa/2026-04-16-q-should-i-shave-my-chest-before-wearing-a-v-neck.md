We are big advocates of v-neck tees. The style feels youthful and a little more laid back that than the inherently more conservative crews. Chest carpet, however, can be an issue with the ladies. According to a poll we posed recently, 81.4 percent of women surveyed said very hairy guys are not appealing. (Remember when hirsute heroes like Magnum PI and 1960s James Bond were sexy symbols of virility? Watch a few seconds of Magic Mike and it’s obvious what the modern version of sexy is.) But, that doesn’t mean ladies want you as sleek as a hairless cat. Women tend to like their men between somewhere between hairy beast and smooth operator, so you'll want to take down your shrubbery to a little longer than a half inch.

Some key points to remember:

You can always go shorter, but can't make it re-grow instantly, so don’t get scissor happy and end up with something patchy and weird that looks like you have a medical condition. Also, when hair is shorn too short, it tends to itch when it grows back in. Since vigorously scratching one’s chest is generally frowned upon in most settings—especially when you’re on stage—don’t get too aggressive with the trimming implement.

[Before Wearing V-Neck](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/)

There are two options: using clippers with a safety guard or sharp scissors. With clippers, find the ideal setting and test out a patch first. (We like the Phillips Norelco Body Groom Shaver.) With scissors, a comb and your fingers, trim that mess by brushing up a section of hair, placing two fingers at the base of the hairs and holding with your thumb, and cut away the excess. Check back every 2-3 weeks. If you want some advice on great trimmers and shave gels, check out our Grooming Guide for other options.
