What Are Peptides?

Peptides are chains of amino acids. This makes them somewhat similar to proteins, which are also chains of amino acids. However, peptides are smaller chains.

Peptides play a big part in many of your body’s processes. They are released by some parts of your body to activate a response in other parts. They can also serve in other roles, like acting as antioxidants.

As people age, the number of peptides within their bodies starts to decrease naturally. So, many people choose to fight this change with peptide therapy. Therapy like this helps replenish peptide levels. In some cases, it can even be used to help direct the body in a specific way.
