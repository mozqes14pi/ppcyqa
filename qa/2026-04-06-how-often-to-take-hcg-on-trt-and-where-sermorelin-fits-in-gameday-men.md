What Is HCG and Why Is It Used with TRT?

Human chorionic gonadotropin (HCG) is a hormone that mimics the function of luteinizing hormone (LH). In a healthy male body, LH is released by the pituitary gland and signals the testes to produce testosterone and sperm. When a man begins TRT, his body senses the influx of testosterone and reduces or stops natural LH production. Without this signal, testicular size may shrink, and sperm production can decline. This process is known as the negative feedback loop. (3)

HCG helps solve this problem by stepping in as a substitute for LH. By stimulating the testes in a similar way, HCG allows men on TRT to maintain testicular function and fertility.In other words, while TRT provides the testosterone you need, HCG can keep the body’s reproductive system active. HCG can be used alongside TRT for a variety of reasons, such as: (4)

Preserving fertility by maintaining sperm production

Preventing testicular shrinkage

Supporting natural testosterone production in conjunction with TRT

For men who want the benefits of TRT without sacrificing long-term reproductive health, HCG is an important part of the equation.

How Often Should You Take HCG on TRT?

The frequency and dosage of HCG depend on your individual needs, goals, and lab results, and your provider will work with you to determine the best treatment plan for you. In most clinical protocols, HCG is administered two to three times per week in low, divided doses (typically 250–500 IU per injection). This helps mimic the body’s natural hormone rhythms and avoids over-stimulation of the testes.

Several factors influence the right schedule for you, including:

Fertility goals: Men who want to actively preserve or enhance fertility may require higher or more frequent doses.

Response to treatment: Some men respond quickly to HCG, while others may need adjustments.

Lab results: Bloodwork helps determine how well the body is responding to TRT and whether HCG levels need fine-tuning.

It’s important to note that misusing HCG can create risks. Taking too much can desensitize the testes, leading to reduced effectiveness over time. Improper use may also lead to more severe side effects. Common side effects from HCG may include headache, fatigue, and mood changes, while less common but more severe effects may include allergic reactions, blood clots, or unexpected hair growth. (5)

Because of this, it’s essential for men not to attempt to self-prescribe HCG. The right dosing schedule can only be determined through medical supervision and ongoing monitoring.

The Role of Sermorelin in Hormone Optimization

While HCG supports the testosterone side of hormone health, Sermorelin addresses another key piece of the puzzle: growth hormone. Sermorelin is a peptide that stimulates the pituitary gland to release more of your body’s natural human growth hormone (HGH).

HGH plays a vital role in sleep, energy, metabolism, recovery, and overall well-being. Unfortunately, HGH levels decline as men age, often contributing to fatigue, slower recovery, and weight gain. Sermorelin helps counteract this decline by encouraging your body to produce HGH naturally. Sermorelin is preferred by most medical professionals over direct HGH injections due to a safer profile and fewer adverse effects. (6)

When used alongside TRT and HCG, Sermorelin can offer a complementary effect. TRT restores testosterone levels, HCG maintains testicular health, and Sermorelin boosts growth hormone production. This combination helps men achieve a more complete hormonal balance.
