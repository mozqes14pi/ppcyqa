Explanation

Doctors start by checking your nail and asking about any problems you have noticed. They might gently scrape a bit of the nail or the skin around it to send to a lab. In the lab, experts use tests like a KOH exam or even grow the fungus in a culture to see if it is present. New tools like special cameras or even computer programs can help check for fungus too. When it comes to treatment, many fungal nail infections are treated with antifungal medicines. Sometimes the medicine is put directly on the nail as a cream or lacquer, but if the infection is more severe or widespread, doctors may prescribe pills that you take by mouth. The treatment can take a long time because nails grow slowly. Keeping your nails dry and trimmed and following your doctor’s advice will help treat the infection.
