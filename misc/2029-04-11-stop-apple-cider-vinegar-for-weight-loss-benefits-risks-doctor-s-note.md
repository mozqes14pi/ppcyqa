What are the potential benefits and risks of using apple cider vinegar for weight loss?

Internal Medicine

Apple cider vinegar (ACV) is often promoted as a natural weight loss aid. Research suggests it may support modest weight loss by improving blood sugar regulation, slowing digestion, and increasing feelings of fullness. However, ACV can also cause side effects such as stomach upset, nausea, tooth enamel erosion, and low potassium levels, especially when consumed undiluted or in large amounts. It may also interact with certain medications, including insulin and diuretics.

Because weight changes, digestive issues, and blood sugar concerns can stem from many underlying causes, it's important to understand what's actually driving your symptoms before trying home remedies like ACV.
