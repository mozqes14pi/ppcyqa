The Rock does everything big — and that includes his paydays.

Dwayne Johnson, as he's known on his financial reports, is highest paid actor on the Forbes list of celebrity earners for 2020, and the tenth highest paid celebrity in the world. It's a boost from 2019, when the actor ranked at number 15 on the same list. He currently ranks beneath Kanye West, Tyler Perry and LeBron James, among others.

And of course he's still getting paychecks for his work on his sitcom Young Rock, and hosting The Titan Games.

The actor's long-standing career has commanded him a large fortune, and with his constantly growing filmography, it doesn't look like The Rock plans to stop earning millions of dollars each year.

In 2018, The Rock earned an income of $124 million.

Forbes declared that Johnson's $124 million 2018 income from his movies and Ballers HBO series was the largest ever recorded for acting in the 20 years the publication has tracked celebrity earnings.

Unsurprisingly, Johnson took to Instagram to commemorate the news, echoing his typical refrain of thankfulness for his success and his commitment to hard work.
