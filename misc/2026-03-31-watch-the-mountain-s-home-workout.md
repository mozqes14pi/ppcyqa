# Watch The Mountain's Home Workout

Hafthor Björnsson, a.k.a. The Mountain won the Arnold Strongman Classic 2020 back in March just as the United States began to respond to the coronavirus (COVID-19) pandemic. The strongman might not have the World's Strongest Man contest to train for in his immediate future—the event was postponed from May to November— but he's not letting quarantine prevent him from finding a record to smash. He's currently working on beating fellow strongman Eddie Hall's 500 kilogram deadlift. (The two strength titans are currently beefing about the upcoming attempt, with Hall objecting that Björnsson will be pulling at his home gym.)

That means that, like so many other people, Björnsson is now training at home. He starts his home workout with weighted lunges in the hallway and living room of his house. And then, he shows off how he's set up shop for the first time in n his garage to get his workouts done.

"First time squatting here," he says, showing off his new rack. "We're able to do so much on this rack. Squatting, bench press, incline press... even yesterday we did rows."
