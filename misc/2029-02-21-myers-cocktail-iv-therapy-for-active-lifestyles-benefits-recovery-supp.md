How Myers’ Cocktail IV Therapy Supports Active Individuals

Whether you’re working out regularly, training for a local race, or simply trying to stay energized and well, the Myers’ Cocktail offers a range of potential benefits:

Rapid Rehydration

Physical activity leads to fluid loss through sweat, which can affect recovery and performance. The Myers’ Cocktail replenishes fluids and electrolytes quickly by bypassing the digestive system and going straight into the bloodstream. Studies suggest IV rehydration may be more effective than oral methods in certain cases of dehydration.[¹]

Energy Boost

The B-complex vitamins and B12 in the infusion play a vital role in converting food into fuel, reducing fatigue, and supporting sustained energy levels for day-to-day activity or fitness routines. Research has shown that B vitamin supplementation can enhance energy metabolism and reduce fatigue in physically active individuals.[²]

Muscle Recovery

Magnesium and calcium are essential for muscle relaxation, contraction, and reducing cramps. Post-exercise, they may help ease soreness and inflammation. Magnesium, in particular, is known to lower markers of muscle damage after strenuous activity.[³]

Immune Support

Physical stress from regular exercise may temporarily weaken immune defenses. Vitamin C boosts the immune system and has been shown to reduce the severity and duration of respiratory illnesses, especially in individuals under physical stress.[⁴]

Reduced Inflammation

Workouts can lead to oxidative stress, which delays recovery. Antioxidants like vitamin C and glutathione (optional) neutralize free radicals and support quicker healing and reduced inflammation.
