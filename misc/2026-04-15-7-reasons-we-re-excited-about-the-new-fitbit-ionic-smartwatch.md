# 7 Reasons We're Excited About the New Fitbit Ionic Smartwatch

[New Fitbit Ionic](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

Prepare to do way more than just count your steps: Fitbit's new smartwatch is finally here, and it’s called Ionic. It's the brand's first, and it tracks your heart rate, takes a look at how you're sleeping—or aren't—and tells you how many calories you torched during your workout. You can even see texts in between sets without having to get your phone out of your gym bag.

"It's not that we're entering smartwatches for the sake of entering smartwaches," said James Park, CEO of Fitbit, at an event last week. "We feel strongly that smartwatches are a platform for us to deliver the most advanced health tools that the market has seen. The larger form factor of smartwatches allows us to integrate more advanced sensors, richer graphics and a display, with amazing battery life."

You’ll have to wait until October to get one for your workout (it's available for pre-order now), but here are a few reasons why we're looking forward to Fitbit's new gear.

It’s a music-lover’s device

Why? You can sync it up to a set of wireless headphones to listen to 300 songs. Yeah, 300 songs. (Right on time, the brand also unveiled Fitfit Flyer, a pair of Bluetooth workout headphones that come with a variety of earpieces to tailor to the fit of your ears. And we're always psyched about new workout buds.) It works with Pandora (the Pandora you have to pay for). If you’re someone who needs tunes during a workout but doesn’t want to hold your phone in your hand and risk dropping it, or sticking it in the pocket of your shorts, this is a potential game-changer.

It knows when you need a quick—or long—break

Sometimes you gotta stop on a run. Whatever the reason, the watch, when you’re using the Run mode, will automatically pause when you’re not moving. Go again and the clock starts. Plus, you can track your route when you’re finished thanks to the GPS capability. You'll also be able to see your time even if it's a bright day thanks to the screen's display.

Buy a cup of coffee with it

The Ionic makes it a lot easier to get caffeinated. There’s a Starbucks icon waiting for your tap that will be built right into the device. Set up your credit card inside the Fitbit app on your phone for Fitbit Pay. Buy your cold brew with your wrist, and you’re out the door.

You'll only feel a little guilty about missing your workout class

That’s thanks to Fitbit Coach, one of the Ionic’s new features that takes you through a workout—showing people actually doing the movements and giving you time to do the exercise, too—right on your wrist. It's similar to that of the Blaze. Fitbit also says it’s going to release Audio Coaching next year.

It won’t need constantly charged

Few things are as annoying as reaching for your tracker or any gadget and realizing it’s out of juice again. Fitbit claims the Ionic will keep up with you for over four days until you need to worry about where you left that charger. But if you’re in the zone with your playlist or running with GPS—or both—then that number will be closer to 10 hours, according to the brand.

You can wear it in the deep end…

Really. Fitbit Ionic can work up to 50 meters, which means you can swipe to a swim workout on the watch, hop in the pool, and get in your laps. Or at least rest easy knowing it won’t get ruined in the shower or at the beach.
