What might cause knee pain when pressed on?

Knee pain you feel when pressing on it might come from problems like bursitis, tendinitis, arthritis, extra fluid build-up, or injured tissues around the knee.

What might cause pain around the rib cage both in the front and back?

Pain around the rib cage both in the front and back is commonly caused by muscle strain or injury but can also be related to inflammation of the cartilage, conditions affecting the nerves that run along the ribs, or problems affecting the organs near the rib cage .

What might cause pain in the urinary tract?

Pain in the urinary tract typically occurs when bacteria or irritants cause infection or inflammation in the bladder, kidneys, or urethra.

What might cause pain on the left side of the body, affecting both the arm and leg?

Pain on the left side affecting both the arm and leg can come from problems in the brain or nerves, such as a stroke or central nerve pain.

What might cause pins and needles all over my body and how can it be managed?

Pins and needles all over your body might be due to nerve problems such as sensory neuropathy, which can sometimes be linked to conditions like diabetes, and it can be managed by treating the underlying cause along with supportive care.

What might cause pins and needles all over the body that comes and goes?

Pins and needles that come and go can be caused by small nerve problems, changes in blood sugar, or temporary nerve compression, and you should talk to a doctor if they occur frequently or are associated with other symptoms.

What might cause random body vibrations and when should I seek medical advice?

Random body vibrations, often felt as tremors, can be caused by things like stress, muscle fatigue, or conditions that affect the nerves, and you should see a doctor if the vibration is persistent or worsens.
