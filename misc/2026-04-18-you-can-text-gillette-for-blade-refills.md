# You Can Text Gillette For Blade Refills

Can’t be troubled to go out and buy blades? Gillette gets it. Now all you have to do is send a text. Gillette is letting guys re-up on razor cartridges with its newly revamped shave club: Gillette On Demand. After you’ve signed up and made your first order (new customers get $3 off), just text “blades” to the word BLADES (252-337) to automatically refill your order, which you’ll receive in two to three business days.
