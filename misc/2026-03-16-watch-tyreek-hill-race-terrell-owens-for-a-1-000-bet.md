# Watch Tyreek Hill Race Terrell Owens for a $1,000 Bet

[Race Terrell Owens](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/)

Tyreek Hill, the 26-year-old Kansas City Chiefs wide receiver known as "Cheetah," has not slept on his training during quarantine. Hill has showed off his workouts, including some tough hamstring moves and tempo runs.

So when he was challenged to a race by one of the best wide receivers in the league from years ago—Hall of Famer Terrell Owens, a.k.a. TO, who is 46 years old—Hill couldn't miss a chance to defend his status as the fastest player in the NFL. After all, Hill did talk about wanting to try out for the 2020 Olympics back in January.

Former wide receiver Chad "Ochocinco" Johnson, 42, also joined the guys to watch the race.

The race had a $1,000 wager. The speedsters met up at a local high school track and football field to do the run.

"Once I plant, I'm gone," says TO as he warms up.

"This is the Olympics right now, man!" Hill adds as he took to the field for warm ups.

For the first race, the guys (joined by TO's nephew) run the length of a football field, with Hill giving Owens a 10-yard head start. Owens won, but then told Hill he wanted to race him in a "head-on" 40-yard dash. And the results were pretty outrageous.

Hill finished slightly ahead of Owens, clocking in at 4.37, while Owens clocked a 4.42. While these were hand times, which aren't as accurate as the laser timing systems used for tests like the NFL Combine, this is still very impressive.

[Terrell Owens](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/7785585237.html)

"I'm in that 4.4 range. Chad, I'm that dog. You want to be my agent?" says Owens after the race.

"You got it by just a smidge," Owens says to Hill. "I can pick 'em up and put 'em down with the best of 'em."

Once the clip was published, the internet was loving every second.
