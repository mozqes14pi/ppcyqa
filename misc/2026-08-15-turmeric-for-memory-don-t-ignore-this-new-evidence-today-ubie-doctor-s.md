Turmeric is a spice that contains curcumin, which has been studied for its potential benefits on brain health. One study found that curcumin might help people with Alzheimer's disease by reducing certain brain changes associated with the condition. Another study suggested that curcumin could enhance mood and working memory, which is the ability to hold and use information in your mind. However, not everyone may experience the same benefits from curcumin. Some people might notice improvements in memory and cognitive function, while others might not see much change. If you're experiencing concerning memory issues or cognitive changes, consider taking a free symptom checker assessment to better understand your symptoms.
