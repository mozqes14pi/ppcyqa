While preparing for a couple of upcoming cold weather trips (such as an excursion to the Arctic Circle,) I was excited to discover this awesome collaboration between Tommy Hilfiger and Rossignol, the iconic winter sports gear brand. The collection perfectly pairs the bold graphic aesthetic of the American fashion label and the high-performance technology of the French sportswear brand for hitting the slopes or apres ski lounge.

The collection offers pieces with technical performance fabric providing incredible stretch, durability, quick-drying, and waterproof-coating and seals to keep you comfortable for all your winter activities. Key items like the chevron ski jacket have breathable honeycomb-like padding that protects the body’s microclimate from outside influences, providing optimal comfort. Plus, the thoughtful construction of a slim-silhouette with a removable hood that can fit over any ski helmet is a major bonus that skiers will appreciate.

Beyond the stellar ski jacket, the collection includes all the clothing layers a man could need to conquer freezing temperatures for winter sport activities. Style and function go hand-in-hand in this collection featuring a red, white, and blue color palette found in both brands’ logos. Wear a the full look or just one of the jackets with your favorite blue jeans and white sneakers—either way, you can’t go wrong.

Whether you’re planning to hit the highest ski slopes or just pound the pavement of your cold city streets, this collaboration is worth both your attention and hard-earned dollars.
