These tests reveal whether inflammation is present, how severe it is, and whether treatment is working. **Key inflammation markers in children:** - **CRP (C-reactive protein):** Rises quickly with acute infection or inflammation - **ESR (erythrocyte sedimentation rate):** Reflects longer-term inflammation - **Procalcitonin:** Helps distinguish bacterial from viral infections - **Ferritin:** Elevated in severe inflammatory or autoimmune conditions Interpretation depends on trends over time, clinical symptoms, and follow-up testing—not a single result alone. Because inflammation markers overlap across many childhood conditions, understanding your child's specific symptoms is essential before ordering tests or planning care.
