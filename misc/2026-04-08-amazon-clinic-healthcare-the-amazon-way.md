# Amazon Clinic: Healthcare, the Amazon Way

Amazon Clinic, the company’s equivalent of a drop-in doctor’s office, offers on-demand consultations with licensed doctors and nurse practitioners. With an online sign-up portal, users can select the condition they need to address; pick a provider to work with (listed with estimated wait times); and, after working with that clinician, receive a prescription, if needed, to treat the ailment.
