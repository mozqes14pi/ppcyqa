What Bioidentical Hormone Therapy Is

Bioidentical hormones are compounds that are structurally identical to the hormones the human body produces, such as estrogen, progesterone, and testosterone. Bioidentical hormone therapy uses these to help address symptoms that may be associated with declining or imbalanced hormone levels.

The word bioidentical refers to the molecular structure, not a promise of a particular outcome. Whether therapy is appropriate, and in what form, is a clinical decision based on your symptoms, your health history, and your lab results.
