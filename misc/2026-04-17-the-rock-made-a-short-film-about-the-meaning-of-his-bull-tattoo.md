# The Rock Made a Short Film About the Meaning of His Bull Tattoo

Sure, you can smell what The Rock is cooking, and you probably even know why he’s got a five-and-a-half pack (thanks to an old wrestling injury). But even the more careful watcher of Mr. Dwayne Johnson may still wonder: Hey, what’s the story with all of those tattoos?

If that’s you—or you’re just generally interested in the art of tattooing—The Rock’s Instagram has a compelling teaser for an upcoming short documentary tracing the evolution of Johnson’s body art. Titled “The Evolution of the Bull,” it follows Johnson’s work with well-known tattooist Yomico Moreno. The two have spent the better part of a year collaborating on a bull tattoo, a revision of work Johnson had previously.

[Bull Tattoo](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/2013816658.html)

[Rock Made](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/5194223207.html)

As he’s detailed in previous Instagram posts, Johnson has been working with Yomico for more than thirty hours of tattooing, “pretty challenging as my whole upper arm and shoulder wasn’t a blank canvas but rather enhancing and adding to what was already there so the level of detail, precision and specific color for my skin takes a lot of time,” as he put it in one caption.

[the Meaning](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/4984538727.html)

[the Meaning of His](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/5648525871.html)

Now, he’s ready to reveal the finished product. Or, almost—the actual short documentary arrives Thursday on Johnson’s Instagram. Judging by the teaser, it’s more than just a making-of video. It opens with somber music and very serious-looking Rock. Over images of his still-progressing tattoo, his family, and his body, the 49-year-old Johnson waxes reflective about how the work has made him think about who he is and what kind of legacy he wants to leave behind. The process appears to have been a true journey for him, and this Thursday he’ll reveal just where it’s taken him.
