# This Simple Doorframe Hack Will Let You Set Up a Resistance Band Anywhere

Australian bodybuilder and YouTuber Eugene Teo just shared a simple, easy hack to set up resistance bands in your home when you don't have anything sturdy and stable to attach them to. "Today I'm going to be showing you guys a cool home hack that will let you set up bands anywhere at home where you have a door," Teo says at the start of the video.

[Band Anywhere](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/)

Fit Simplify Resistance Loop Exercise Bands

Turns out, all you need is a cotton sock. According to Teo, this will be used as your anchor point. He uses a Stance sock (aka, a longer sock), and ties a knot at both ends. "We're going to pull them as far as we can out towards the edges to give you some room in the center (of the sock)," he says.

[This Simple](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/0670938743.html)

From there, you simply go over to your door and open it slightly, placing the sock between it with both knots finding placement outside of the door as you close it. This will create a small loop in the sock. Close the door fully and lock it to create more resistance in the loop. "Now we have something we can attach our bands to," Teo says, as he loops his resistance band through the sock.

Now you can set the sock up at different heights to create different angles for your anchor point. "You can set it up super high, and you can set it up super low," Teo says. "This guarantees you'll be able to hold in place when you do any of the chest exercises or back exercises or shoulder exercises or leg exercises that you want using your bands."

However, if you don't trust the integrity of your sock, Teo says you can just swap it out with a leather lifting strap. To use that, loop the strap through the hole like you normally would to strap it around your wrists. Then, tie it off with a knot, creating the loop to put your band through. Open the door, place the knot outside of it, and you're ready to go.
