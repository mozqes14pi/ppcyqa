About this lesson

Enhance your expertise in lip hydration and contouring with this comprehensive training video, designed for medical professionals seeking to refine their aesthetic skills. The video offers a detailed step-by-step guide, covering everything from assessing lip condition and addressing specific concerns such as wrinkles and dehydration, to the precise application of Belotero products for optimal results. Learn how to use advanced injection techniques to hydrate the lips without adding volume, achieving a natural and glossy finish. This video provides you with an expert-led approach to performing subtle, effective treatments, ensuring your patients experience beautifully enhanced lips with smooth, hydrated skin. Through careful technique and attention to detail, you’ll be equipped to offer a superior aesthetic service, creating elegant, youthful lip contours that meet and exceed patient expectations.
