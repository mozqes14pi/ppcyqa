What is Pityriasis Rubra Pilaris?

Pityriasis rubra pilaris is a group of rare skin disorders that cause a reddish-orange discoloration of the skin and the appearance of scaly patches with well-defined borders. It typically affects elbows, knees, palms, and soles.

Typical Symptoms of Pityriasis Rubra Pilaris

Black/blue/brown spots or moles, on the skin and lining of the mouth

Hard or stiff skin

Dry and flaky skin

Change in nail shape

Skin problem on the chest, tummy or back

Diagnostic Questions for Pityriasis Rubra Pilaris

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are there black, blue, or brown spots on your skin? (Including moles)

Have you noticed any hard or stiff areas on your skin?

Do you have dry and flaky skin?

Did your nail shape change?

Are there any skin problems on your stomach, chest, or back?

Treatment of Pityriasis Rubra Pilaris

Pityriasis rubra pilaris usually requires a combination of prescription creams and oral medications.
