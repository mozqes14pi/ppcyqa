What to Expect Following Juvéderm Treatment

Patients often want to know what changes to anticipate immediately after treatment and in the following weeks. While results are visible quickly, the full effect develops over time:

Immediate volume enhancement and reshaping in treated areas are often visible right after the procedure.

Mild swelling or tenderness may occur and typically resolve within a few days.

Minor bruising can appear depending on the injection site and technique used.

Most patients begin to see more settled, natural results within 10 to 14 days post-treatment.

During the first 24 to 48 hours, it’s important to avoid strenuous exercise, excessive heat, and sun exposure.

Filler continues to integrate into the tissue over several days, contributing to a smoother, more blended look.

Final outcomes are best evaluated at the two-week mark, when swelling has fully subsided.

Temporary lumps or unevenness may be felt but usually resolve on their own as the product settles.

Some tightness or firmness in the area is common and typically fades within a few days.

Treated areas might look slightly overfilled at first due to swelling and water retention.

Full tissue integration may take up to 30 days, especially in deeper planes like the cheeks or jawline.

Patients should postpone facial massages or skin treatments for at least two weeks unless advised otherwise.

Results can be prolonged with good hydration, skincare, and avoiding sun damage or smoking.

FAQs

Is it worth getting Juvéderm?

Yes, Juvéderm is worth it for many patients looking for non-surgical facial enhancement with minimal downtime. Results are natural, safe, and can be tailored to individual goals.

Who should not use Juvéderm?

Individuals with severe allergies to hyaluronic acid, active skin infections, or certain autoimmune conditions should avoid Juvéderm. Pregnant and breastfeeding patients are also advised against treatment.

Which fillers last 2 to 5 years?

Most Juvéderm fillers last 6 to 18 months, but products like Juvéderm Voluma XC or Juvéderm Volux XC may last up to 2 years in some cases. There are currently no FDA-approved hyaluronic acid fillers designed to maintain results for as long as 5 years. Most last between 6 months and 2 years, depending on the product and treatment area.

What happens when you stop getting Juvéderm?

If you stop using Juvéderm, your face gradually returns to its baseline appearance as the product dissolves naturally over time. There are no long-term negative effects.
