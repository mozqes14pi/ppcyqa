# Chris Pratt Helped a Terminally Ill Teen Complete His Bucket List

Let's face it: As much as we love Chris Pratt, Chris Hemsworth, Chris Pine, and Chris Evans, it can be hard to tell the Hollywood actors apart. Besides having the same first name, they all star in our favorite superhero and sci-fi movies; their fitness games are all on-point; if you blurred your eyes and looked at the four of them, you'd be hard-pressed to tell which Chris was which.

To help you keep track of everyone's favorite Chrises, we're rounding up what each of them got up to during the week. Welcome to This Week in Chrises.

Chris Pratt: Made a terminally ill fan’s dreams come true

In a Wednesday Instagram post, Pratt shared that he was spending time with one of his biggest fans, Jacob Monday.

"This is my new pal Jacob Monday. @j8ke.monday He is a 16 year old kid from upstate New York with a unique and powerful story," Pratt explained. "He was diagnosed with a rare form of bone cancer. He recently decided to stop treatment and is currently checking things off his bucket list. Needless to say he and his family have been through a lot."

One thing Monday wants to do is get a bunch of Instagram followers. "Show the love," Pratt wrote to his followers.

Pratt also shared a link for Monday’s GoFundMe page, which is currently raising money to help fulfill more of his wishes now.

Chris Evans: Shared the moment he met his dog

On Wednesday, the country celebrated National Pet Day – a very real and very important day – by flooding social media with photos of their animals. No matter how cute your post was, there's no way it stacks up against the video Chris Evans shared.

"This is the moment we met," Evans tweet, along with a video of the moment he met his pooch in a shelter. "He was trying so hard to stay seated even though he desperately wanted to get out. I knew right away that he was coming home with me, so I took this video to always remember our first hello. Rescue dogs are the best dogs!! #NationalPetDay."
