Iron IV therapy is an infusion that delivers iron directly into your bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system. Because the iron doesn’t have to pass through the gut, it’s absorbed faster than with oral supplements. At Drip Hydration, we use Venofer, a clinically trusted form of intravenous iron designed for optimal absorption. Every infusion is administered in your home, office or hotel by a licensed registered nurse, so you receive hospital-grade care.

Following iron IV therapy from Drip Hydration, many clients notice meaningful improvements across several areas of their health. Better oxygen transport can produce a general “reviving” effect. People often describe feeling more alert and energized within weeks of treatment. Other benefits can include improved exercise tolerance, enhanced cognitive function, stronger hair and nails and a more resilient immune system.

What can Iron IV therapy help with?

Our in-home Worcester iron IV therapy addresses symptoms and conditions linked to iron-deficiency anemia. It’s commonly used to treat fatigue, low energy, dizziness, chronic headaches, brittle nails and pale skin. Some people also choose IV iron treatment to reduce the symptoms of restless leg syndrome. Because iron deficiency can mimic other conditions, we always recommend getting routine bloodwork before treatment. These lab tests include ferritin, hemoglobin, and a complete blood count (CBC), all of which help our medical team design a protocol matched to your specific needs.

Does Iron IV therapy have side effects?

Iron IV therapy is generally well tolerated. If side effects occur, they tend to be mild and temporary. These may include nausea, headache, slight dizziness or minor discomfort at the injection site. More serious reactions, such as allergic responses or gastrointestinal distress, are rare but can be discussed with your Drip Hydration care provider before your appointment. A registered nurse is present throughout your entire infusion to monitor your response and address any concerns in real time.

How often should I do Iron IV therapy?

The ideal frequency will depend on the severity of your iron deficiency and your overall health goals. Is Iron IV safe in pregnancy?

IV iron is sometimes used during pregnancy when iron deficiency is detected and oral supplementation isn’t effective. However, it should only be pursued with the direct approval and oversight of your physician or OB-GYN. If you’re pregnant, please inform your Drip Hydration nurse when scheduling your appointment so we can coordinate your care appropriately.
