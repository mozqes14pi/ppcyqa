Clonazepam is a prescription medication sometimes used to treat Sleep-Related Rhythmic Movement Disorder (RMD), particularly in severe cases when behavioral approaches, safety measures, and other first-line strategies have not provided enough relief. As a benzodiazepine, clonazepam may help reduce the repetitive movements—such as body rocking, head banging, or head rolling—that occur during sleep or drowsiness, potentially improving sleep quality for both the individual and household members. However, because clonazepam carries risks including dependency, daytime drowsiness, and side effects, it is only prescribed under close medical supervision after other treatments have been considered.

If you or a loved one is experiencing unusual movements during sleep, understanding the possible causes is an important first step.
